Christmas, sorted. Nobody forgotten, nothing to remember, and nobody has to sign up.

SECRET SANTA WITHOUT THE EMAILS
Create a group, share one card, draw names. Everyone types their name and gets their own private link, so there's no chasing addresses and nothing to install. When the names are drawn, each person opens their own envelope and sees only their match, with that person's wishes right there. Add people yourself if you know everyone, or share the code and let them join. Close the door once everyone's in.

WISH LISTS, ONE LINK
Say what you'd love with a photo, a link, or just words. Share one link and the people buying for you can claim a gift without seeing what anyone else chose. You never see it.

LISTS FOR YOUR KIDS
Keep a list for someone who can't make their own, and give the other parent an edit link so you build it together from two phones. On a child's list both parents see who's getting what, so nobody double-buys.

FAMILY CIRCLES
Everyone's lists side by side. Invite with a code or a link, no accounts. Show a circle only the lists you choose.

MY CHRISTMAS LIST
Everyone you're buying for, what you've bought, what's wrapped, and what it cost against your budget. Private to you.

YOUR CARD IS YOUR KEY
Every Secret Santa gives you a card to save. Opening it on any phone brings everything back: your exchanges, your lists, your circles. Add an email when you want the same on every phone without a card. No password, ever.

XmasGoat works in the app and at app.xmasgoat.com. An internet connection is required.
