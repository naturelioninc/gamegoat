Remember who you’re buying for, what they want and what you already bought.

MY CHRISTMAS LIST
Keep everyone you’re buying for in one place. Save gift ideas, set a budget, record prices and mark gifts purchased or wrapped.

WISH LISTS
Write down what you’d love, add web links and share your list. Friends can open a shared link without an account. Save someone’s list to the person you’re buying for. Reserve a wish to help avoid duplicate gifts, while keeping the surprise from the list owner.

SECRET SANTA
Create an exchange, invite people with a link or QR code and draw names privately. Join with a code, scan a QR code or choose a QR photo. See your assigned recipient’s wishes and track their gift in My Christmas List.

ONE ACCOUNT, PHONE OR WEB
Your account keeps your lists available in the Android app and at app.xmasgoat.com. An internet connection is required. Shared wish lists and supported invitations open on the web too.

Need inspiration? Follow a gift-ideas link to XMASGOAT.COM. You can organize all your gifts without shopping through the app.
